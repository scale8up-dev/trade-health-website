For employers

Getting started takes one conversation.

Book a 15-minute fit call

We’ll ask about your crew, what you offer today and what you’re trying to solve. If it isn’t a fit, we’ll tell you.

Start with a 90-day launch

Sign a membership agreement and pay your first month. Confirm renewal and cancellation terms on your fit call.

Add your crew

Set up your roster in the employer portal.

Pay one monthly bill

One flat fee per enrolled worker. Manage your roster as your crew changes.

Review the results

At 30, 60 and 90 days, review activation and use in totals, never individual medical details.

For employees

Care without leaving the jobsite.

Check your phone

You’ll get a text or email from TradeHealth with a sign-up link.

Activate your private account

Sign up right in your phone’s browser. Nothing to download.

See a licensed provider

Connect by phone or video with a medical provider licensed in your state.

Get treated

When clinically appropriate, your provider can send a prescription to your chosen pharmacy.

Follow up as needed

Ongoing medication management and check-ins are part of your membership.

Privacy built in

Your employer pays for access. Your health stays between you and your provider.

Employers see

The roster, the monthly bill and approved group-level reporting. Clinical records are handled separately from employer membership administration.

Employers never see

Who had a visit, why, what was prescribed, or any medical record.

Everyday care

A simple path to a provider.

Talk with a licensed provider about common illnesses, ongoing conditions, medication questions and wellness guidance. Your provider determines which concerns can be treated virtually and when another level of care is needed.

Know when to go elsewhere

What the membership isn’t.

  • Emergencies: call 911 for life-threatening symptoms or serious injuries.
  • On-the-job injuries: use your workers’ compensation carrier.
  • Hospital stays, surgery and specialist care: use other care resources.
  • Insurance claims: TradeHealth does not file or process them.
