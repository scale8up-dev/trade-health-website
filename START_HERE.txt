FOUNDER RATE — REQUIRED BUSINESS RULE
Standard employee membership: $59 per employee per month.
Founder Rate: $49 per employee per month for the first 100 enrolled employees
IN EACH STATE. The threshold counts employees, not employers. Once a state
reaches 100 enrolled employees, the special closes in that state and standard
pricing applies to new enrollments. The current mockup displays the offer but
DOES NOT track enrollment or automatically withdraw it.

Before production, obtain the owner's decision on rate duration: whether an
employee who qualifies keeps $49 after the threshold, for how long, and on what
renewal terms. The current copy deliberately makes no rate-lock promise.
Also confirm handling of crews that cross the remaining state allocation,
new hires, cancellations, re-enrollment and employee state attribution.

Implement an authoritative per-state eligibility allocation in the enrollment
backend, with atomic updates so simultaneous enrollments cannot exceed the cap.
Do not use browser storage, lead-form submissions or a guessed counter as the
enrollment source of truth. Use the same availability source for enrollment,
offer messaging and calculator eligibility. Define whether closed state offers
remain closed after cancellations. Hide/replace the applicable offer when full.
No live remaining-spots count is currently shown.

PRICING CALCULATOR
Offers a Founder Rate versus standard rate comparison.
Spouse add-on: $20 per employee/month; family add-on: $35.
Current illustration totals:
  Employee: founder $49 / standard $59
  Employee + Spouse: founder $69 / standard $79
  Employee + Family: founder $84 / standard $94
Add-ons are unchanged from the earlier supplied website copy; obtain final
confirmation of eligibility and terms. Annual estimates assume 12 months at
the selected rate and are not a commitment to a 12-month Founder Rate lock.
The calculator assumes the same tier for the whole crew and is not an
allocation or enrollment engine.
