Prices and Founder Rate estimates

Published standard pricing is $59 per employee per month, with a $20 spouse or $35 family add-on. The $49 employee Founder Rate is limited to the first 100 enrolled employees in each state, not 100 employers. Availability, rate duration and renewal terms are confirmed before enrollment. An inquiry does not reserve an allocation or guarantee eligibility.

The calculator is an illustration, not a quote, enrollment system or live availability check. It assumes the same option and selected rate for your whole crew. Annual figures assume 12 months at that rate and do not promise a 12-month Founder Rate lock.
